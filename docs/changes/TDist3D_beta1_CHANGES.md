# TDist3D beta 1: changes (8 November 2026)

First version. Roger, 7 November 2026: "can we do a scaled down rotatable version of that 3D t-distribution plot in PPL/MicroPython?" The plot is the one drawn in Maple for *Statisticles*, Figure 9.3: Student's t density f(t; ν) as a surface over t and the degrees of freedom ν.

## What it is

- **The surface, in PPL throughout.** f(t; ν) over t = −6…6 (25 points, every 0.5) by ν = 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 (11 rows at their true ν on a linear axis), drawn as a `LINE_P` wireframe with BiNorm3D's orthographic projection and drag (0.6° a pixel, 8-px dead zone, φ 10°–80°). Colour by height, blue to magenta to red, on a fixed scale with 0.4 at the top. The normal curve in gray dots just past the back edge, labelled N(0,1), ν = ∞. A coarse 13 × 6 grid while a finger drags (Menu can turn it off), the full 25 × 11 when it lifts.
- **Why no MicroPython.** A frame is about 510 `LINE_P` calls and a projection of 275 points; Python would have to hand hundreds of numbers back across the bridge every frame (BurrLab beta 1e's lesson). Nothing in the program is slow enough to need it: the surface never changes, so every height, every segment colour, the ghost row, and the Curves view's eleven curves (about 1,500 `STUDENT` calls) are cached once at the start.
- **The slice.** One row ν₀ in red with its numbers in the corner: the peak f(0; ν₀), t crit = `−STUDENT_ICDF(ν₀, 0.025)` (the mirrored form), and P(|t| > 2) = `2·STUDENT_CDF(ν₀, −2)` (a doubled lower tail), each beside the normal curve's 1.960 and 0.046. Red posts at ±t crit when they fall inside t = ±6. ν− and ν+ (also the − and + keys) climb a ladder from 0.5 to 1,000; Menu > Set ν exactly takes any value from 0.5 to 1,000 (two decimals kept; outside the range refused with a sentence, nothing changes). Above ν = 10 the slice is past the back edge and the corner says so.
- **The Curves view.** The family seen end on: eleven curves coloured by ν from blue to red, N(0,1) in black dots, ±1.960 in gray dots, the slice in bold red with ticks at ±t crit. Up and Down move the slice; a tap picks the curve nearest the finger at that column.
- **Soft keys** ν− · ν+ · Curves/3-D · Slice · Reset · Menu; Esc quits. **Menu:** set ν exactly, the views, slice, reset, grid while dragging, frame timing (G1 test), help (four pages with the fleet header "TDist3D help n/4: Topic"), quit. **Home export** TD_NU, and a one-line RETURN summary.
- Layout: readouts top right, the angles bottom left, a one-frame message bottom right (the corners the surface leaves clear at the starting angles); 15 help lines at a 12-px pitch, 46 characters at most; every TEXTOUT_P carries a width.
