# Sensible ranges for what a student types, and menus that fit (round 6, 2 November 2026, night)

*Roger, 2 November 2026, after BurrLab's S was capped at 1,000: "cap at 1,000 is just right--what about in our other programs? Maybe we need to check for "reasonable" caps on data a student would enter". On alpha: "Well for alpha we dont need like 0.00001 or something—use real world values like 0.001, 0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 and so forth". And: "Also, some of the menu items run out of the box". This is how the 30 programs were probed, what was changed, and what was left open on purpose.*

## The rules

1. **A value a student types is refused with a sentence, never changed without a word.** Several forms used to clamp: StudRange made an alpha of 5 (meant as 5 %) into 0.5, and the plot's k of 25 into 20, with nothing on the screen. A refusal says what the range is and changes nothing.
2. **Alpha is from 0.001 to 0.2 everywhere**, AnovaLab beta 1b's range: the real-world values Roger named (0.001, 0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2) and the ladders the arrows step through. A drag on a critical line stops at the same ends. An area that is not an alpha (NormExplorer's "x at a percentile") keeps 0 to 1.
3. **A cap goes where the program stops being right or readable**, not at a round number for its own sake: where a table cell stops showing a number whole (EpiStats and ChiTable, 9,999,999; TrendTest, a level's total of 99,999), where an algorithm loses its digits (StudRange's df, 100,000, which the program already used for infinity), where a printed table ends (StudRange's k, 100), or where a value stops meaning anything (an STR allele's repeat number, 1 to 99).
4. **Values in the data's own units stay open**: means, standard deviations and data values. A student's data can be in grams or in dollars. The programs that cannot draw a value refuse it already (round 4: NormExplorer, StatDist, PowerViz, SampDist, ScatterLab, TTestLab).
5. **A menu item is at most 30 characters.** The menu box (`CHOOSE`) sizes itself to its longest item up to about 214 px and cuts what is longer (Virtual Calculator, 2 November 2026: BurrLab's box 214 px with items of 38 characters, 191 px once they were shortened). That is round 2's rule, which five programs had slipped.

## The probe

`tools/caps_probe_v1.py PROGRAM.txt CALL --runs 40 --seconds 12 --lists --out NAME.json` drives a program with the monkey (`tools/monkey_v1b.py`), but at every form it meets it types ONE field with a value from a ladder (1,000,000, 1,000, 1E9, 0, 1E15, −1, 10,000, 100,000, 0.5, 1E7, 1E12, 1E100) and leaves the other fields as the form shows them, so whatever happens next belongs to that one value. The run's timeline says whether the program refused it (a message box that reads like a refusal before the next form, or the same form again), went on, crashed or froze. `tools/summarize_probe_v1.py FOLDER` puts the programs' results in one table and lists the fields worth a look: a value of 1,000,000 or more accepted, a crash or freeze after a typed value, a value outside the range the field's own help line states.

Over the 30 programs (round 5's 29 and BurrLab beta 1l), 40 runs each: 239 fields reached (the fleet has about 180 `INPUT` calls; forms deep in a program are reached less often). 128 took 1,000,000 or more. Most of those are open by design (rule 4). Already guarded: CICatcher (n 2 to 100, p 0.01 to 0.99, sigma against mu), ChiTable (counts to 9,999,999), AgreeLab (tiles to 99,999), ErrorRate (a database of 1 to 100,000,000), BayesTree (the root 10 to 1,000,000), SurvLab (at most 100 life-table intervals), TrendTest (cases between 0 and the level's total, whole numbers).

## The checks

Each changed program has a verify script, `verify/verify_<Name>_<version>.py`, with its output beside it: 156 checks, all PASS (123 in the ten round-6 scripts, BurrLab beta 1l's 21, beta 1k's 12). Its X part types the out-of-range values and the edges into each changed form in the harness and checks the refusal sentence, that nothing is computed, that the edges are taken, and that the old version took the same values without a word. Its R part runs the monkey, 30 seeds, on the old version and the new one: every run takes the old version's path, except where the new version refused a value with one of this round's sentences (the script prints each such seed) or a message is rewritten on purpose (ChiTable's), and its D part finds no new overlap, straddle, overrun or cut number. Every menu item is read out of the source and measured.

Two things the checks found on the way, both fixed before shipping:

- **StudRange's df.** The first build, beta 15h, had the alpha and k caps only. Its verify run showed beta 15g stopping with a division by zero when the monkey typed a df of 1E300 into Tukey HSD; tried by hand, a df of 1E12 gave a wrong q crit. Beta 15i caps df, and its X6 check shows q crit at df 100,000 equal to the infinite-df value to four decimals.
- **TrendTest's CI column.** `tools/text_overlap_v1` put the 95% CI 3.5 px over the results page's frame at its narrower width estimate (5.5 px for every character). At the measured widths (a decimal point is 3 px) it ends at x = 313.5, inside the frame at 315, and the Virtual Calculator showed every CI of the snoring example inside it. The verify script judges that edge at the measured widths and says why, as BurrLab beta 1l's does for its y numbers.

## Left open

- Values stepped with the arrows or a drag are not typed, so the probe does not reach them. BurrLab's x0 and S were capped by hand (beta 1k). A static scan found no other stepped value that multiplies its own step without a cap, but a hold-the-arrow test of each explorer on the Virtual Calculator is still to do.
- The probe types one field at a time. A combination of two fields, each in range, that the program cannot draw is the monkey test's to find (round 4 found several), not this probe's.

## How to rerun

```
python3 tools/caps_probe_v1.py programs/StudRange_beta15i.txt "StudRange()" --runs 40 --seconds 12 --lists --out probe/StudRange.json
python3 tools/summarize_probe_v1.py probe/
cd verify && python3 verify_StudRange_beta15i.py
```

A program whose main routine is not its file's name is called by its own: `NormExp()`, `POWERVIZ()`, `TTESTLAB()`, `TTESTLABG()`, `DATALOAD()`, `Norm3(0,1,1.96)`.
