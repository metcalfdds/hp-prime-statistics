# From the book

The book *Statisticles* (Roger D. Metcalf, 2026) is the companion to the programs in this repository. On 26 November 2026,
in its Draft 106, the book was refocused on its two purposes, to illustrate statistical concepts to beginning students and
to show what the HP Prime can do. What left the book is kept for the book's future website, and the programming part comes
here (Roger, 26 November 2026: "The programming part can go to GitHub and a future website").

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Appendix_F_For_HP_programmers_Draft105.md` | Draft 105's Appendix F, "For HP programmers", as Draft 105 printed it: where everything is, writing PPL with an AI assistant, where to find a feature, what is in each program, how the Sudoku bonus works inside, what we learned about the calculator, the programs that measure a calculator, how we tested, and where else to look, with the works it cites. Its section "PPL, and the HP languages before it" is now the only home of that history: the book carried it on in Appendix E as "From key presses to PPL" until Draft 110 (26 November 2026) took it out, word for word as here except for four references to Appendix E. Its Chart Gallery is at the front of the current draft (Figure G.1). |
