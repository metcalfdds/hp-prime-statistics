# Extras

Programs and files that go with *Statisticles* but are not among the thirty-one *Statisticles* the book teaches with. They are here for readers who want to go further. Each one is a teaching and demonstration aid, like everything in this repository, and none is validated casework software.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| `fdaes/` | **FDAES, the Forensic Dental Age Estimation Suite** (v3.9d, 4 November 2026), an advanced HP Prime program that runs seven published dental age methods and pools their answers. It grew out of Roger D. Metcalf's Fellowship in Forensic Dentistry at the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio and the Bexar County Medical Examiner's Office. `FDAES_v3.9d.txt` is the program (paste it into a new program named FDAES in HP's Connectivity Kit). `FDAES_Student_Guide_v1e.md` is its guide. `FDAES_v3.9c_CHANGES.md` and `FDAES_v3.9d_CHANGES.md` say what changed in the last two versions. `verify_FDAES_v3.9b.py` and its output check the program's arithmetic against Python; the script was written for v3.9b and hard-codes the folders of the session that ran it, so it needs editing before it runs elsewhere. |
| `maple/` | **The Maple t surface**: Student's *t* density drawn as one surface over *t* and the degrees of freedom, the Maple worksheet behind Figure 9.3 of *Statisticles*, which TDist3D draws on the calculator. See its own README. |

Read the disclaimer at the top of the FDAES guide before you use FDAES.
