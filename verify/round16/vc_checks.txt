   titled "Dunnett d crit: about 14-41 s on a G1", 37 characters, and stood as "Dunnett d crit: about 14-41 s o"
   (AnovaLab_FIRST_BUILD_title_cut).  "Dunnett: about 37-112 s on a G1", 31 characters, stood whole, and the box of
   the cut title was the narrower of the two (243 pixels against 256): the limit is a count of characters, not a
   width.  Beta 1h's "Tukey q crit: about 14-41 s on a G1" is 35 characters and must have been cut the same way since
   it was written.  MENDED: the four boxes give the time without "about"; the longest title is 31 characters, and all
   four were seen whole on the file as filed (AnovaLab_dunnett_ask, _ask_dcrit, _ask_tukey_q, _ask_tukey_p).  The fact
   is in tools/prime_facts_v1.py (fact 4), and the five verify scripts of this round check every title they see (D3).
   No literal title in the 31 programs is longer than 31 characters (160 CHOOSE calls read: 128 literal titles, the
   longest 30 characters; 32 titles are built at run time or held in a variable, and were not all exercised).

b. What a program returns is shown, after a run from the Program Catalog, in a box of five lines of about 26
   characters; the rest is not shown.  CICatcher's first closing line for a tolerance run, "CICatcher: 107 of 111
   tolerance intervals held at least 95 % of the population (96.4 %) at 95 %, tol. 95 %, mu = 100, sigma = 15, n = 10;
   see CQ_COVER", ended in the box at "sigma =" (CICatcher_FIRST_BUILD_closing_cut).  MENDED: "CICatcher: 105 of 111
   held at least 95 % of the population (94.6 %) at 95 %, n = 10", four lines, seen whole for 111 and for 1,011
   intervals.  The other closing lines seen this evening fit their boxes.

c. Key presses that do nothing.  About one press in eight was lost on the programs' pages; KeyProbe counted it
   (keyprobe_r16.txt beside this file).  A loop that polls GETKEY loses presses on this calculator and WAIT(-1) loses
   none.  Nothing was changed for it in this round: whether a G1 or a G2 loses presses is not known.
